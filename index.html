Shinsei: Rebirth

A trace of cultivated peace, offered as refuge, and perhaps a doorway into practice.

Shinsei is a series of sumi-e paintings and hanging scrolls. Each one is made from meditation and Reiki practice, within a Buddhist and Shinto frame. The work begins inward. If something in it reaches you, that is a consequence of the practice, not its aim.
