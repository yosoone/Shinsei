Writings

Research notes that sit behind the series. Full texts will be added as each one is finished.

  • Shinshō: Mind-Nature心性

    Follows the word 心性 from early Indian teachings on the luminous mind, through Chinese Buddhist and Confucian thought, into Japanese debates on original enlightenment, and asks what it means for a painting practice that takes the name.

    Research note, in preparation

  • Usuzumi Ink薄墨

    Pale ink in condolence etiquette and in ink painting, the Usuzumi-zakura of Gifu, and what diluted sumi offers the series.

    Research note, in preparation

  • Gold in Spiritual Arts and Scriptures金

    Five qualities of gold across Buddhist, Christian, Islamic, Jewish, Hindu, Daoist and Egyptian traditions, and the traditions' own doubts about it.

    Research note, in preparation

  • Gold and Indigo in Sacred Art紺と金

    Why gold written on deep blue recurs from Japanese indigo-paper sutras and Tibetan blue-black manuscripts to the Blue Qur'an.

    Research note, in preparation
